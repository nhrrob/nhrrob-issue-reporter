# 2026-11-23: the Aurora redesign (0.4.0)

## Where things stood

0.3.0 was released on 2026-11-11. #15 (redesign with mockups), #16 (search
and filters), #17 (Pro PRD), #18 (market check) and #19 (WordPress.org) were
open.

## What was done

- **Design** (#15): on the design canvas the first options reused the
  older plugins' shell and were rejected as basic; the owner chose
  **Aurora** from three premium looks, with its dark variant as dark mode. All screens were drawn in it, including the planned
  UAT widget and Inbox. The files are in `docs/design/`.
- **Market check and plan for a paid add-on** (#17, #18): done in a private
  repository. The widget, annotation and Inbox are free elsewhere, so they
  come to this plugin.
- **0.4.0**: the app rebuilt in Aurora, light and dark: shell, New report,
  Sent and Settings, with the filters and search of #16 (not yet in the
  abilities). Checked in a browser on the dev site in light, dark and at
  390px wide, with sample reports and GitHub answered by a temporary
  `pre_http_request` filter that was removed afterwards.
- **WordPress.org**: 0.4.0 merged and released; the blog took it as
  1.33.3. Then prepared the submission: Plugin Check clean on the zip's
  files, the readme rewritten for the new screens, `admin/src` and
  `package.json` in the zip, icon, banners and six screenshots, Plugin
  Check on pull requests, and releases by hand as in Post Insights: a new
  tag deploys to WordPress.org, a push to `trunk` updates the listing.
  Security and performance were reviewed with nothing to fix.

- **0.4.1**: the site's name under "Report Issue" in the sidebar instead of
  "This site"; the banner's card centred; screenshots taken again.

## Left open

- "This site" under the title is the live 0.4.0; #23 (0.4.1) fixes it.

## Later: made public

  and the paid add-on's plan were taken out of `.ai/PRD.md`, `.ai/DESIGN.md`,
  `docs/DECISIONS.md`, this note and `docs/design/README.md`; they are in the
